How it works

Quick & Simple
Scanning Process.

01

Scan in the app

Open holograb on your iPhone and walk a slow circle round the plate. A guided ring fills in as you go — about a minute a dish.

02

We train it

Your capture goes to our GPUs, which train a Gaussian splat of the dish. The table, the walls and the floaters are cleaned away.

03

A clean 3D dish, centred and true to colour — on your QR menu, in embeds for your own site, and in posters and videos.
