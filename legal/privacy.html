Privacy notice.

This notice explains what information holograb collects when restaurants use our app, dashboard and hosted menus, why we hold it, and what you can do about it. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

Who we are

holograb is run by [Company name] Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (number [00000000]), registered office [address]. For the information described here we are the controller under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Contact: [privacy@yourdomain].

What we hold

  • Account details — your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store it in plain text), plus your sign-in sessions.
  • Restaurant and menu content — restaurant name, address, logo, dishes, prices, descriptions, allergens and your menu design choices.
  • Dish captures and 3D models — the photos, camera positions and outlines your phone records while you scan a dish, and the 3D models, thumbnails, posters and videos we make from them. Please scan dishes, not people.
  • Menu analytics — counts of menu views, QR scans and dishes opened. These are totals per restaurant; we don’t build profiles of your guests.
  • Billing records — your plan, subscription status and the Stripe customer reference. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Technical logs — IP address, device and browser type and error logs, kept for security and to fix problems.
  • Messages — what you send us, such as a custom-build request or a support email.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you signed up for: capture, processing, hosting and publishing your menu (contract).
  • To take payment and keep accounting records (contract and legal obligation).
  • To keep holograb secure, prevent abuse and improve how it works (legitimate interests).
  • To reply to enquiries and send essential service emails. We will only send marketing if you opt in, and you can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell personal data. We share it only with providers that help us run holograb, under contracts that protect it:

  • Stripe — payments and billing, as our payment processor.
  • Hosting and processing — [hosting provider] stores the service and your content; dish captures are processed on GPU machines run by us or [GPU provider].
  • Google Fonts — our pages load typefaces from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Professional advisers, and authorities where the law requires it.

Anything you publish — your menu, dish models and embeds — is public by design.

International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum. [Confirm per provider.]

How long we keep it

  • Account, restaurant, menu and model data — while your account is open.
  • Raw scan captures — up to [90 days] after processing, so a dish can be re-processed, then deleted.
  • Backups — roll off within [30 days].
  • Billing records — six years, as UK tax law requires.
  • Technical logs — up to [30 days].

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Dashboard → Account or in the iPhone app. This permanently deletes your account, restaurants, menus, scans and models; published menus and QR codes stop working. Copies in backups disappear as those backups roll off. We keep billing records for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Email [privacy@yourdomain] — we reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and storage

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. When you sign in, the dashboard keeps a sign-in token in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and access to your restaurant needs your sign-in. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you and the ICO where required.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we’ll tell you by email or in the dashboard before it takes effect.
